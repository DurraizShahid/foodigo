# Multi-Vendor Food Delivery System: Features Roadmap

This document outlines a comprehensive features roadmap for a multi-vendor food delivery system. The features are categorized by the primary user of the application: Customer, Restaurant (Vendor), Driver (Rider), and the platform Admin.

---

## 1. Customer-Facing App (Web & Mobile)

### Core Features
- ✅ **User Authentication:** Secure sign-up and login (email/password, social login).
- ✅ **Profile Management:** Manage personal details, addresses, and payment methods.
- ✅ **Restaurant Listings:** Browse restaurants with filters (cuisine, rating, price, distance) and search functionality.
- ✅ **Real-Time Order Tracking:** Live map tracking of the driver's location and order status updates (e.g., Preparing, On the way, Delivered).
- ✅ **Menu & Ordering:** View restaurant menus, customize items (add-ons, special instructions), and add to cart.
- ✅ **Secure Payments:** Payment gateway integration UI (Stripe, PayPal) - ready for backend integration.
- ✅ **Ratings & Reviews:** Rate and review restaurants and drivers.
- ✅ **Order History:** View past orders and easily re-order.
- ✅ **Push Notifications:** Push notification system with debug panel - ready for backend integration.

### Advanced & "Cool" Features
- ✅ **AI-Powered Recommendations:** Personalized restaurant and dish suggestions based on order history and user behavior.
- ✅ **Group Ordering:** Allow multiple users to contribute to a single order.
- ✅ **Scheduled Orders:** Place orders in advance for a specific date and time.
- ✅ **Subscription Services:** "Uber One" or "DoorDash Pass" style subscription for free delivery and exclusive offers.
- ✅ **Gamification:** Loyalty programs with points, badges, and rewards for frequent orders.
- ✅ **Voice Ordering:** Voice ordering card with integration ready for voice assistants.
- ✅ **Social Features:** Share favorite restaurants or meals with friends on social media.
- ✅ **Dietary & Allergen Filtering:** Advanced filters for dietary needs (vegan, gluten-free) and allergen information.

---

## 2. Restaurant Partner App/Dashboard

### Core Features
- ✅ **Restaurant Profile Management:** Manage restaurant information, hours, and contact details.
- ✅ **Menu Management:** Create, update, and manage menu items, including descriptions, prices, and photos.
- ✅ **Order Management:** Accept/reject incoming orders, manage order status, and view order details.
- ✅ **Earnings & Analytics:** Dashboard with insights on sales, top-selling items, and customer feedback.
- ✅ **Promotions Management:** Create and manage discounts, special offers, and deals.
- ✅ **Payout & Transaction History:** View transaction history and manage bank details for payouts.

### Advanced & "Cool" Features
- ✅ **Inventory Management:** Real-time inventory tracking to automatically mark items as "out of stock."
- ✅ **Dynamic Pricing:** AI-powered suggestions for price adjustments based on demand and competitor pricing.
- ✅ **Heatmaps:** See where most orders are coming from to understand customer density.
- ✅ **Customer Insights:** Detailed analytics on customer demographics and ordering patterns.
- ✅ **Direct Chat with Customers:** Communicate with customers directly regarding their orders.

---

## 3. Driver/Rider App

### Core Features
- ✅ **Driver Profile & Registration:** Onboarding process with document verification.
- ✅ **Availability Toggle:** Set status to "online" or "offline" to receive orders.
- ✅ **Order Notifications:** Receive and accept/reject delivery requests.
- ✅ **GPS Navigation:** In-app navigation with optimized routes to the restaurant and customer.
- ✅ **Earnings Tracker:** Real-time tracking of earnings per delivery and daily/weekly summaries.
- ✅ **In-App Communication:** Chat with the restaurant and customer.
- ✅ **Order Status Updates:** Update order status from pickup to delivery.

### Advanced & "Cool" Features
- ✅ **Route Optimization:** AI-powered route planning to handle multiple deliveries efficiently.
- ✅ **Earnings Boost & Incentives:** "Quest" or "Surge" pricing to earn more during peak hours.
- ✅ **Instant Payouts:** Option to cash out earnings instantly.
- ✅ **Performance Analytics:** Insights on delivery times, acceptance rate, and customer ratings.
- ✅ **Vehicle Selection:** Choose vehicle type (bike, car, truck) to get appropriate orders.
- ✅ **"Hotspot" Notifications:** Get notified about areas with high order demand.

---

## 4. Admin Dashboard (Platform Owner)

### Core Features
- ✅ **Vendor Management:** Onboard new restaurants, manage listings, and set commission rates.
- ✅ **Driver Management:** Manage drivers, track their performance, and handle payments.
- ✅ **Customer Management:** View user data, manage support requests, and handle disputes.
- ✅ **Financial Oversight:** Comprehensive analytics on revenue, profits, and payouts.
- ✅ **Content Management:** Manage promotional banners, featured restaurants, and other content on the customer app.
- ✅ **Support & Ticketing System:** Manage and resolve issues from customers, restaurants, and drivers.

### Advanced & "Cool" Features
- ✅ **Advanced Analytics & BI:** Integration with BI tools for deep insights into platform performance.
- ✅ **Fraud Detection System:** AI-powered system to detect and flag fraudulent orders or activities.
- ✅ **Multi-City/Country Management:** Tools to manage operations in different geographical locations.
- ✅ **Marketing & SEO Tools:** Built-in tools for running marketing campaigns and optimizing for search engines.
- ✅ **A/B Testing Framework:** Test new features and UI changes on a subset of users before a full rollout.
- ✅ **Fleet Management Integration:** For businesses that want to manage their own fleet of drivers.
